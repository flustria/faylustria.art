About the Artist

Hi, my name is Fay and I'm a 22 year old aspiring artist and student-athlete based in Los Angeles, CA and Portland, OR. Being self-taught, I'm still currently developing my style, but actively work in a variety of mediums including acrylics, oils, mixed media, and digital art. I'm primarily drawn to and inspired by nature, animals, and people, but am open to exploring other art subjects to broaden my skillset.


Whether I’m creating a personal piece or a custom commission, I love making artwork that feels meaningful, warm, and one-of-a-kind!
